# The 500 (working name)

August 30, 2026. Supersedes compound-idea.md, which Marlon rejected.
One line: learn your way through the S&P 500 one company at a time,
and your XP becomes your stake in the ones you know.

Mock: public/sketches/fivehundred.html (playable, phone frame,
12 companies standing in for the 500).

## The loop, as Marlon called it

- The tree is companies, not concepts. Sectors are branches, each
  node is one company. Duolingo's path, but every node is a real name.
- A lesson is short: what the company does, one story that sticks
  (McDonald's is a landlord, Costco sells memberships not groceries,
  Viagra was a heart drug), one check question, then the price and
  what you pay per $1 earned in plain words.
- Finishing pays flat XP. XP is the only money. 50 XP buys $50 of
  any company you have learned so far, fractional shares fine.
- You can only ever own what you have learned. The investable
  universe grows node by node.
- Sell anytime, it becomes XP again, rebalance freely.
- Day over day the portfolio moves on real closes. The overnight
  banner is the reason to come back, the next node is the reason
  to stay.

## Why this might be the one

- 500 companies is a year and a half of daily content from one bake.
- The collection instinct does the retention work: know 37 of 500.
- The stories are the curriculum. P/E, moats, dividends and business
  models arrive attached to names people already care about.
- Investing != gambling lives in the structure: you cannot buy what
  you cannot explain, and there is nothing to day-trade because new
  money only arrives by learning.
