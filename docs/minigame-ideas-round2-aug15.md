# Minigame ideas, round 2, August 15 2026

Brief: hypercasual loops where money is the material, not the lesson. One verb, zero tutorial, fun first, teaching only as a side effect, viral share moment designed in. Six isolated scouts, one game tradition each. Replaces the rejected curriculum-first list in minigame-ideas-aug15.md.

## Family: guess the price (calibration)

- **Ballpark** (S): one absurd real documented price per day (auction lot, medical bill, parking spot), one typed guess, share card is your tick vs truth on a log scale plus the crowd's median error. KEEPER.
- **Sold!** (M): real closed house listing, four guesses with higher/lower + warm/cold feedback, arrow-trail share card. Everyone already plays this at dinner. KEEPER.
- **Spot On** (S): a needle sweeps a dollar scale, tap to stop it on what the item really costs; sweep speeds up with streak. Keeper, overlaps Pour Decision.
- **Pour Decision** (S): tilt-pour clinking coins into a jar, stop the pour at the item's price; momentum makes stopping a twitch skill. Keeper, overlaps Spot On.
- **Piggy Sense** (M): shake a porcelain piggy, coins thud/tinkle per real masses, guess the contents, smash to find out. Daily pig, shatter freeze-frame share. KEEPER, most original of the family.

## Family: inflation / time travel

- **Checkout** (S): a real vintage receipt, three guesses to date the year, earlier/later arrows; the receipt itself is postable. KEEPER.
- **Time Machine** (M): two purchases from different decades, tap which cost more in today's dollars, streak survival; share the versus card that killed you. KEEPER, the higher-or-lower ask directly.
- **Mall of 1989** (M): dropped into a real year with $50 and a scanned catalog, grab a haul before the register closes; polaroid haul share with "same cart today" twist. KEEPER.
- **Two Big Macs** (S): 2048 slide-merge where merging two identical products advances them a decade at the real price; end card is the price ladder you personally merged. Keeper.

## Family: scale of big money

- **Stacked** (M): guess how many burritos fit in Judge Judy's salary, then the real counter blows past your guess and piles into a skyscraper. Share the pile with your tiny "my guess" marker. KEEPER.
- **Tall Money** (M): Stack-style tap-to-drop swinging bundles of hundreds, climbing toward the true physical height of a real price (median house = 26 ft of hundreds). Keeper, overlaps Dead Weight.
- **Dead Weight** (M): drop $10k cash bricks onto a scale until it balances a real object; camera pull-back shows true scale. Keeper, overlaps Tall Money.
- **Everything Must Go** (S): 60 seconds to liquidate a real billionaire's actual holdings to $0 by tapping SOLD; garage-sale flyer share ("left him with $11,406 and a Honda Civic"). KEEPER, most viral energy in the pool.

## Family: exact amounts (dexterity + arithmetic)

- **Zero Out** (S): add real store SKUs to a cart until the total hits today's target to the penny; share is the printed receipt with a NAILED IT stamp. KEEPER.
- **Penny Jar** (S): coin Suika — drop real coins, touching clusters that sum to a bigger coin fuse (5 pennies snap into a nickel), forge toward a gold dollar. KEEPER.
- **Exact Change** (M): tap coins from a till to make change before the customer's patience empties. CUT: closest of the pool to feeling like math homework.
- **Riffle** (S): hold to riffle bills off your thumb, release at exactly $10,000. CUT: satisfying toy, not enough game.

## Family: daily deduction

- **Same Difference** (S): Connections, but the 16 real things group by secretly costing the exact same price ("a latte = a shotgun shell??"); classic colored-grid share. KEEPER, strongest single concept in the pool.
- **The Ringer** (M): a real menu/receipt/shelf reproduced faithfully with exactly one price doctored — tap the liar. KEEPER.
- **Pay Stub** (S): a real-composition pay stub reveals line by line, guess whose job it is in as few lines as possible. Keeper, most reading-heavy of the keepers.

## Flagged and cut

- **Top Tick** (S): a famous historical bubble draws itself, one tap to sell the top, revealed and graded ("96.8% of Bitcoin's top"). FLAGGED: brilliant and viral but it is Hold's first cousin (one input vs the tape); candidate to become a Hold mode instead of a standalone.
- **Paperclip** (M): Suika trade-up ladder from a paperclip to a used car at real resale prices. Keeper-adjacent; the merge chain is authored rather than discovered.
- **Same Price** (S): match-3 where the match rule is equal price. CUT: weaker twin of Same Difference.
- **Shelfie** (M): daily blind-box object onto a curated shelf. CUT: no crisp win condition.

# Wave 4 (pivot: user rejected price-trivia entirely; "monopoly variant maybe idk")

Diagnosis: waves 2-3 produced quizzes. The liked games have GAME feel — stakes, luck, drama, a fantasy, money physically moving. Wave 4 mined proven money-game DNA: Monopoly, game shows, push-your-luck, money-native board classics. Guess-the-price formats banned outright.

## Monopoly DNA

- **Landlord Lap** (M): "Roll, buy streets, survive rent" — 16-square daily board of one real city's actual streets (Detroit Monday, Miami Tuesday), three laps, buy or skip while three AI rivals buy what you skip and charge real rent. Score is final net worth. STAR, the direct "monopoly variant" answer.
- **Rent War** (M): "Bankrupt the rival landlord first" — one-on-one duel on a famous real street (actual Boardwalk day one, then Rodeo Drive, Abbey Road); FORECLOSED stamp share card. Keeper.
- **Buy the Block** (S): "Buy the block or bust" — door to door down one real street, roll before each address, bank anytime or a burst-pipe/tax-bill roll wipes the unbanked run; deeper doors are cheaper but pit odds climb. STAR, cheapest build of the wave.
- **Going Once** (M): "Outbid tycoons, complete the city" — real landmarks at real assessed values under the hammer vs three AI bidders with hidden bankrolls; values are public so it's bankroll poker, never price-guessing. Skyline share card. Keeper.

## Game-show DNA

- **Lot Thirteen** (S): "Deal or No Deal, estates" — twelve sealed lots from a real celebrity estate auction with real hammer prices; open lots, banker recalculates offers, take the deal or ride. "Banker offered $310,000. I said no. My lot was Cobain's Unplugged guitar: $6,010,000." STAR.
- **Ghost Ride** (M): "Ride a mystery stock decade" — $10k in an unnamed real ticker, each tap advances one real year, banker offers a buyout between years; Enron and Blockbuster are genuinely in the deck; identity revealed when you stop. Natural Guess the Stock sibling. STAR.
- **Big Board** (S): "Press Your Luck, real crashes" — spin tiles that are real single trading days applied to your pot; three are real collapse days (Black Monday, Lehman's last session) that take everything. STAR.
- **Cold Tank** (M): "You are the fifth shark" — five real anonymized Shark Tank pitches, split $500k across invest-or-pass, then documented outcomes grade you ("You passed on the video doorbell. Amazon paid $1B"). Keeper, the suite's skill anchor.

## Push-your-luck

- **Rollover** (S): "Ride a real jackpot's rollovers" — each tap advances one real Powerball drawing in a historical run-up; cash out your cut or hold, but the drawing where someone actually won is hiding in the sequence. Same run daily for everyone. STAR, simplest concept in the wave.
- **Payday** (S): "Draw paychecks, dodge real bills" — Farkle with a checkbook: real BLS paychecks stack unbanked, real bill averages drain, one Wipeout card (real average medical bankruptcy) zeros everything unbanked. Keeper.
- **One More Day** (M): "Hold the bubble, bank gains" — dropped into a real named mania, each tap is one real trading day, crash date is real and waiting. FLAGGED: same body as the specced Hold game.
- **Smash and Grab** (M): "Bag real loot, beat alarms" — a vault of real auction lots (Salvator Mundi $450.3M, Stan the T. rex), every grab spikes the alarm; escape with the haul or watch it shatter. Keeper.

## Board/card classics

- **Open House** (S): "Bid on weird real houses" — For Sale's proven bid-or-pass auction math over a curated catalog of real absurd listings (missile silo, converted church); flip your portfolio against buyer checks; daily comparable net-profit score. STAR.
- **The Bell** (M): "Corner the market, ring bell" — Pit solo: frantic face-down trades vs three AIs until you hold all nine of one commodity at its real spot price. Keeper, most chaotic.
- **Merger** (M): "Grow hotel chains, force mergers" — mini Acquire with real hotel brands and real nightly rates; tiles collide, bigger chain swallows smaller, majority shareholders get paid. Keeper; two systems (tiles + shares) is the simplicity risk.
- **Hammer** (M): "Rig the daily art market" — Modern Art style: real artists, value set by what the table trades, your Kusama is dead money if the AIs pivot to Warhol. Keeper; elegant but the endogenous-value idea is subtle.
