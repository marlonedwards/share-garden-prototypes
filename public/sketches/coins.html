Coins, visual contract

Wave one, real monthly prices from 2019 to 2024, drawn at the constants in docs/coins-spec.md. Flat two dimensional stand-in for the three.js scene, so the heights are the point and the lighting is not.

January 2019 Tap a stack

Rule one. GameStop at $2.84 and Amazon at $86 are on the same ruler. One coin is a wafer, one is a slab, and the ratio of the drawn thicknesses is the ratio of the prices.

Rule two. Press play and watch Nvidia. Its coins never multiply. They get fatter, and the column rises because of it.

Rule three. Sell a share and it lands in the trough as dollar coins, all of them the same size, ten dollars each. The total height on the board does not change at the moment of the trade.

The frame never moves when you trade. Buy all you like and every other column stays exactly where it was. It is set once from what you are worth at the open, and it only pulls back when prices push a column past the top. The space above the stacks is the room they grow into, so press play and watch it fill.
