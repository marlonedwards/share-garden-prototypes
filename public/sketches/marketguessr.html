Guess the stock: one puzzle, three looks

Rules frozen: full unlabeled year, free guesses, hints count against par, real names. Same mid-round moment (two hints bought, two wrong guesses made) in three skins. Pick a direction or mix pieces.

Your five notes, applied

  • The name is gone. The header just says what to do: guess the stock. That also made "Whose year is this?" redundant, so it is gone too.
  • The size hint is now a real number: the company's market cap at the end of the mystery year.
  • The Hint button says Hint and nothing else. All stroke talk is deleted; par is simply a hint count, and fewer is better.
  • You can type the company name or its ticker symbol.

The frozen rules

PieceHow it works
The mysteryOne real company, one real calendar year of daily closes, shown whole from the first second. Axis reads percent from January and the months; no year, no dollars, no ticker.
GuessingType the company name or its ticker symbol. Free and unlimited; wrong guesses shake and join a quiet plain-text line.
The Hint buttonOne button. Each press deals the next hint off the ladder: 1. Widen (a real year of context each side, mystery year shaded). 2. Sector. 3. The year, stamped onto the axis. 4. Price, real dollars replace the percents. 5. Size, the market cap at the end of the year.
ParPar is a hint count, usually 3. Solve with fewer and you finished under par; that is the brag.
The poolCurated icons and infamy, real names, dead companies included. Expands later.
Daily shellSame puzzle for everyone, streaks, share dots equal your hint count, practice archive behind one link. The game carries no name on screen; marketguessr stays only as the working file name.
