The Orb, index fund representations

Nine live mocks at the game's own fidelity, drawn with the same glass shell, contact shadow and speculars as OrbScene. Part 1 asks how the sealed index orb should read on its own. Part 2 asks what an index fund looks like inside the player's orb, sitting next to a single company's color. Click any orb to test the seal.

Part 1 · The sealed index orb

Goal: hundreds of companies, one of everything, fused shut. Not seven slices of pie.
Every variant carries the same weld ring, rivets and refusal animation, so judge the interior only.

Part 2 · The fund inside the player's own orb

Holding: Nova Systems 45%, an index fund 35%, Bitrix 20%. The fund's share must read as "that part is everything at once".
All rendering is 2D canvas, no external assets. Effort estimates assume the existing OrbScene draw pipeline: the interior fills plug in exactly where fillWaves and fillBlobs already live, and the seal treatment extends the existing sealed branch.
