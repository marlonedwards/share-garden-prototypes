The Orb, design mock

The field guide, the mini-lessons, and the marble journey

Three connected systems, drawn at the game's own fidelity. A codex that fills as concepts are met in play, four short vignettes that teach one asset type each before an era begins, and a shelf of glass marbles that records what the player has actually proved. Nothing here is a streak, a badge, or a point.

One

The field guide

Twelve cards. A card is added the first time the player meets the concept in play, never by reading a menu. The card is free. The marble on it is not. Tap the marble to open its card, so the codex and the shelf are one screen and not two competing collections.

Why the trigger matters. Every unlock fires on something the player did or watched, not on a menu visit. That keeps the codex an honest record of the run. If a player never holds three colors at once, they never get the diversification card, and the shelf shows it.
Two

Mini-lessons, two to three minutes each

One asset type per vignette, played before the era that leans on it. Each one busts exactly one misconception and ends with a single quick-check item. Storyboard below is the first one, frame by frame. The other three are compressed to their beats.

Three

The marble journey

A marble is one concept the player has proved, not one era they sat through. It clears when the quick-check item tagged to that concept is answered right. Three rails, twelve marbles, all slots visible from the first minute so the collection has an end.
