Balloons

A holding is a balloon on a string. Its size is its value, so growth inflates and rises, decline deflates and sinks, and a bankruptcy pops. Your portfolio is one hand holding several strings. Everything here is drawn live on canvas, no assets, so the language can be judged in motion rather than in a still.

The trap this metaphor has to dodge: fast inflation must read as thin skin, not as winning. The hero is the patchwork balloon that cannot pop.

1. The foolproof checklist, rendered live

Every claim the metaphor makes has to survive being drawn.
aInflating

The company earns more, so the balloon takes in more air, grows wider and floats higher up its string.

bDeflating

The company is worth less, so air leaves, the skin goes slack and wrinkled, and the balloon sinks lower.

cA hand of five

Your portfolio is one hand. The biggest balloon is the biggest part of your money, at a glance, with no numbers needed.

dThe cold snap

Bad weather cools the air in every balloon at once, so the whole hand shrinks together. Nothing you did caused it.

Then watch them warm back up.
eDeflated, then popped

Left, deflated: small, still round, still yours, can refill. Right, popped: a knotted scrap on the ground that never lifts again.

The scrap stays until you replay.
gA dividend arrives

A little basket of coins is lowered down the string into your hand. The balloon dips slightly, because the coins came out of it.

the hero
fThe index, one balloon stitched from hundreds of patches

It climbs slowly and never dramatically. When a patch fails it goes dark and is stitched over within seconds, and the balloon keeps rising. There is no hole big enough to pop it, which is exactly why it is boring and exactly why it wins.

hBuying and selling

Buying is coins out of your hand and a new string knotted on. Selling is handing that same string to someone else and taking their coins. Nothing is created, it changes hands.

Rendering notes: every balloon is one teardrop path with a radial gradient, a knot, and a swaying quadratic string, so the same twelve lines of drawing code cover a holding, a hype balloon, and the index. Skin state is the only extra channel, taut and pale when a balloon has inflated too fast, slack and wrinkled when it has deflated. The patchwork envelope is a clipped grid fill, which is what makes hundreds of patches cheap to draw and cheap to repair one at a time.
